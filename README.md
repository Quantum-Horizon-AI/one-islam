## Privacy

- Location is used only on-device to calculate prayer times and Qiblah direction. Never sent to external servers.
- No accounts required. No sign-in, no tracking, no ads.
- All prayer time calculations are performed locally on your device.


Privacy Policy
Last Updated: February 28, 2026
One Islam (“the App”) is developed by Leo Lawrence. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your information.
1. Information We Collect
We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The App operates entirely on your device.
2. Location Data
The App requests access to your device’s location solely to:
•  Calculate accurate prayer times for your location
•  Determine the Qiblah (direction to Makkah) for the compass feature
•  Determine your timezone for correct prayer time display
 
Your location data is processed entirely on your device. It is never sent to any server, third party, or external service. Location coordinates may be cached locally on your device to provide prayer times when offline.
3. Device Sensors
The App uses your device’s compass/magnetometer sensor solely to display the Qiblah direction. Sensor data is not stored or transmitted.
4. Notifications
The App may request permission to send local notifications for prayer time reminders. These notifications are generated and scheduled entirely on your device. No push notification servers are used.
5. Data Storage
All app data, including your settings, bookmarks, and cached prayer times, is stored locally on your device. No data is synced to any cloud service or external server.
6. Third-Party Services
The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data collection services. The App does not contain any ads.
7. Children’s Privacy
The App does not collect any data from any user, including children. The App is safe for users of all ages.
8. No Account Required
The App does not require account creation, sign-in, or any form of registration.
9. Changes to This Policy
If we update this privacy policy, the changes will be posted here with a new “Last Updated” date.
10. Contact
If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at:
dark.matter.dev.ai@gmail.com

لا يستخدم التطبيق أي خدمات تحليلات أو إعلانات أو تتبع أو جمع بيانات من طرف ثالث. لا يحتوي التطبيق على أي إعلانات.
